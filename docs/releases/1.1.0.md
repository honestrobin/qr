# QR 1.1.0, 11 October 2026

## What changed

- The robin's balloon shows from the start: "I'm the robin. I check every square." Once there's a
  code, the same balloon says "This code has 625 squares. I checked every one." (the number is the
  code's own). A tap on the robin hides the line or brings it back.
- Keep it plain is a quiet link at the end of the footer instead of a checkbox. The browser
  remembers it, so the robin stays away until someone taps "Bring the robin back".
- The ruler and its numbers are gone, and the grid became small squares, like a code's own.
- The footer no longer says "Made with AI" on every page. It links to the Robin's Code, which says
  how our products are made; the rule and the Code's article 12 changed the same day
  (honestrobin/robinscode#21).

## Promises this release touches

| Promise | How | Ledger |
|---|---|---|
| What's kept on your device | Now two things, both only once chosen: the language, and Keep it plain | Tested |
| "Keep it plain" | One tap, remembered on this device | Tested |
| Every page links to the Robin's Code | It no longer also says "Made with AI"; the Code says how it's made | Tested |

**A guard changed:** the privacy test that lists what the browser keeps now allows `qr-plain`,
and only with the value `yes`. It still fails on anything else kept, typed or not.

## What the maintainer read

He asked for these changes after looking at 1.0.1 on a computer, chose from screenshots of the
options (he took the recommended ones), asked for the rule on saying it's made with AI to change, saw
screenshots of the result, and gave his go.
