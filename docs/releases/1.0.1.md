# QR 1.0.1, 11 October 2026

## What changed

- The robin is on the page from the start, under the code. Before, it came only with the first
  code, so the page didn't show whose product it was, or whose legs the text field stands on.
- Its one line ("This code has 625 squares. I checked every one.") now shows by itself when the
  first code appears, and its number follows the code. A tap on the robin hides the line or
  brings it back. "Keep it plain" hides both, as before.
- The ruler's marks were 1 px to the right of the mat's lines; now they sit on them.

## Promises this release touches

| Promise | How | Ledger |
|---|---|---|
| "Keep it plain": one tick sends the robin away | Its test now also checks the robin from the start, and was renamed | Tested |

No other promise is touched: the change is the robin's place and the ruler's look. The ledger
checker passes.

## What the maintainer read

He asked for both changes after looking at the live page on a computer (11 October 2026), saw
screenshots of the change on a computer, empty and with a code, and gave his go to deploy once the
checks passed.
