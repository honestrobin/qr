# QR 1.9.1, 15 October 2026

## What changed

- **A roadmap,** `ROADMAP.md`, as the Robin's Code asks: what's now (the free tool, made a joy
  to use before anything is added), next (guides beside the tool) and later (a paid option with
  codes you can change after printing and scan counts, going through your own address, a short
  link of ours or your own server; one Honest Robin account for every tool). It ends with the
  free tool staying free and complete. In order, without dates.
- **One sentence under "Can I change the link later?"** in every language: the paid option is on
  the roadmap, with a link to it, and the free tool stays free, with everything it has.
- The README points at the roadmap.

## Promises this release touches

None breaks, and the ledger grows. "Free: no account, trial, ads, watermark, expiry or upsell"
is kept: the sentence states a plan, offers nothing to buy, and the ledger's row now says the page
names the planned paid option once. Two rows are new, both kept by hand: "The free tool stays
free and complete: nothing the page does today moves to the paid option on the roadmap", which is
the Code's "The deal doesn't change" and "You get the whole product" applied to QR; and "Our
plans are public", kept by the roadmap without goals yet, since QR has none published. The two
"Doesn't apply" rows about price and the self-hosted edition now say "until the paid option
exists". The roadmap's "Later" is written as what would be, not what is, and says plainly that a
code on our short link keeps working for as long as we run it.

## What the maintainer read

He asked for the mention ("maybe mention somewhere that QR roadmap is to have an account ... and
support tracking analytics and things like link changes in the paid option? The free option is
always available") and read the roadmap's "Later" text and the page's sentence, in English,
before they went in ("yeah"). After the promise review, two sentences changed from what he
read: the page's second sentence says "stays free, with everything it has" instead of "stays as
it is" (the tool changes often; what holds is what it has), and the roadmap says what leaving
would mean for a code on our short link. He hasn't read the other twelve languages, or "Now" and
"Next".

## What agents checked

- **The translations:** written by Claude, then checked by a second agent translating all twelve
  back into English. It corrected five: the link words in German, French, Dutch and Turkish
  read as a schedule, a project or a bare "plan" rather than a roadmap, and the Korean could be
  read as if the scan counts were what changes after printing.
- **The promise reviewer** read the change against the ledger: nothing shipped breaks a promise.
  On its reading: two sentences in the roadmap's "Later" were promise-shaped and in the present
  tense about a product that doesn't exist, and now say "would"; "Leaving keeps every printed
  code working" couldn't hold as written for a code on our short link, and now says for how long
  it does; the ledger gained the rows above; and the page's second sentence says what holds.
- **The checks** in desktop Chrome, Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone passed on the pull
  request, and the deploy workflow runs them again before it deploys. The languages test fails
  if any page shows a bracket that wasn't turned into a link; the pages were rebuilt from the
  words on the Pi, and the roadmap link is in all 13.
