# QR 1.7.1, 12 October 2026

## What changed

- **Each layout shows its size** under its picture: 1 (18 cm), 4 (8.5 cm), 12 (5 cm), 30 (2.5 cm),
  a little less with a caption, and it follows the caption as it's typed. People who think in
  centimetres can pick a layout straight away.

## Promises this release touches

None. It's how the sheet section reads; the sizes are the ones the sheet already prints.

## What the maintainer read

After 1.7.0 was built, he and Claude disagreed on the sheet section:
- He proposed "1 per page" at a moderate size, plus more counts.
- Claude kept "1" as big as the page allows, with "Just one" under "Choose your own size" for a
  single code at a size you pick.

He asked for a tiebreaker from a fresh agent, told nothing of who proposed what, and said to go
with its pick. It weighed three options: the built one (A), his (B), and size first (C). It
picked A:
- People print a code for a job (a sign, a sheet of stickers), not for a number of centimetres.
- A single code printed from the sheet is usually meant big.
- Four pictures fit one row on a phone.

It asked for one tweak, the size under each picture, which is this release.

## What agents checked

- **On the maintainer's Raspberry Pi, in Chromium, against the local pages:** the sizes under
  the pictures with and without a caption, in English and Japanese, before a code exists and
  after.
- **The sheet test** checks the four sizes, and that they follow the caption.
- The sizes are `{cm} cm` in every language (`{cm}cm` in Japanese and Korean, as the pages
  write sizes elsewhere), so no translation check was needed.

## Shipped together with 1.7.0

1.7.0's deploy stopped before going live. In the checks the deploy runs first, the test that
prints every layout to PDF on A4 and Letter ran out of time. It prints some 60 PDFs, more than its
30 seconds allowed, and it had passed on the pull request's own run. The test now has three
minutes. 1.7.0 went live with this release.
