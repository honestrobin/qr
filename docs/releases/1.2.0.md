# QR 1.2.0, 11 October 2026

## What changed

A look-and-feel review of the whole page, by a second agent, in desktop Chrome, Android Chrome
and iPhone Safari (CI's pictures) and in Chromium at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, in five
languages. The maintainer chose the fixes from screenshots and a mock.

- **The robin keeps quiet while a code doesn't scan.** Its proud line sat right above "Doesn't
  scan", and wit never sits next to an error. It comes back with "Scans fine".
- **Keep it plain leaves no hole:** the answer stands alone in the middle.
- **On phones, the field asks in fewer words** ("A link or any text"), in a narrower cut of the
  same font, so it isn't cut off in any language from 360 px. Under 360 px, some still are.
- **Japanese breaks between phrases:** the headline, the line under it and the line about what a
  code opens mark their phrase ends; Chrome finds the rest itself. Korean breaks between words.
- **The print-size line** is drawn in whole pixels, so its two ends match.
- **A tall window in one column** no longer leaves an empty band above the code.
- **The robin's balloon is drawn like the language picker:** the robin's cream and outline, with a
  tail to its head, and its lines break evenly.
- **One column width:** the robin's row, the buttons and the note under them are as wide as the
  sticker's cut line.
- **The buttons:** Download PNG and Download SVG side by side, Copy (or Save or share) below them
  at full width. They're in that order for the keyboard too.
- **The 404 page** has the robin and a link to the other tools.
- **Each stylesheet and script is asked for by its own version,** so a page never meets the
  stylesheet of another release while Cloudflare's cache catches up (it did for a minute after
  1.1.1).
- CI now pictures Keep it plain and the page in Korean, and no picture taps the robin.

## Promises this release touches

None. The colours test now also checks that the robin's line is hidden while the code doesn't
scan, and the robin's test that Keep it plain keeps no room for it.

## What the maintainer read

The review's findings with screenshots, and a mock of the balloon, the column and the buttons. He
chose to build all of the mock, saw screenshots of the result, and gave his go.
