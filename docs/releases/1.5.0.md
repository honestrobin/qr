# QR 1.5.0, 11 October 2026

## What changed

The maintainer tried QR on his phone and said what didn't work. This release is his list.

- **The name at the top stands out.** It blended into the mat and its dots. It's now in full ink
  on a patch of plain mat, with QR on a dark tag of its own, and the mat's squares are paler.
- **The robin and its line stand just above the field,** after the first lines of text, on phones
  and computers. The field keeps its two legs; on a phone the field came first and the robin much
  later, so it wasn't clear whose legs they were.
- **Less decoration:** the white shine on the language bubble and on the field read as clouds,
  and the sticker's dashed cut line and scissors weren't liked. All three are gone. A picture
  dragged onto the sticker still shows an outline.
- **Print a sheet and Colours and image are buttons,** with an arrow that turns when they open;
  as plain text they were hard to read on the mat.
- **Keep it plain is gone,** in all 13 languages, so the robin stays. A choice to keep it plain
  made on a device before is forgotten when the page opens. Its count ("qr kept plain") is off the
  list, and the server code refuses it.
- The robin with its QR badge is drawn exactly as it was. Making, checking and taking a code work
  as before.

## Promises this release touches

| Promise | How | Ledger |
|---|---|---|
| One thing is kept on your device, and only once you choose it: the language you picked | Was two things; Keep it plain went, and a choice kept from before is forgotten when the page opens | Tested |
| The counts carry the page's language; nothing else about it leaves your device | Reworded: they no longer say when Keep it plain is turned on | Built, no test |
| By default we also count what's done on the page, never what's in it | One count fewer; a test pins that the old one is refused | Tested |
| Keep it plain | The row went with the setting | Removed |

**Guards changed:**

- `tests/qr.spec.js::the robin says who it is, counts each code's squares, and Keep it plain sends
  it away on this device` is now `…::the robin says who it is and counts each code's squares, and
  stays`. Its Keep it plain half became a check that the button is gone and that a choice stored
  before is forgotten. It also checks now that the robin stands above the field, and that neither
  moves on any device when the robin's line changes (before, only on desktop).
- `tests/privacy.spec.js::what's typed is never put in the page's address or kept in the browser`
  allows one stored key (`qr-language`) instead of two: stricter.
- `tests/count.spec.js`: the pinned list loses "qr kept plain", and a test checks that it's
  refused.
- `tests/qr.spec.js::counts go to the page's own address` expects two counts where it expected
  three, because it no longer turns Keep it plain on.

**A feature went without notice.** The Code says a feature you use is never removed without 90
days' notice. QR has no users yet whom that notice would protect, and the maintainer asked for it
to go now. That's a rule since this release (robinscode #23): until a product has users, a notice
period doesn't hold up a change the maintainer asks for, and the changelog says what changed.

**A rule changed with it.** The rules said every product with wit has Keep it plain. They say now
that every product people work in has it; a free tool someone uses for a minute may go without
(robinscode #23).

## What the maintainer read

His feedback from his own phone is the list above. When the field's legs went in a first version,
he asked for them back, with the robin moved before the field. He saw
screenshots of the phone page before and after, decided that Keep it plain goes and that notice
periods don't hold before there are users, and approved the rule change and the release without
reading the code. His audit of the live page comes after this record.

## What agents checked

- **The checks** on pull request #16's last commit (`964aa4f`; run
  [38141823701](https://github.com/honestrobin/qr/actions/runs/38141823701)): 235 passed in
  desktop Chrome, Chrome on a Pixel 7 and Safari on an iPhone 15, axe's accessibility scan
  included; 61 skipped by design. One test that times the code's redraw on the iPhone took 122 ms
  against 100 and passed on its second try, as it did on 1.4.0's deploy. The change doesn't touch
  the drawing.
- **A second agent's promise review** of `964aa4f` found no broken promise and every changed guard
  stricter or the same, and asked for this record. It noted one edge: the missing-page (404) page
  loads no script, so an old Keep it plain choice is forgotten only once the main page opens. That
  page has no robin, so nothing shows.
- **The same reviewer read the rule change** and said its first wording would have let any
  promise give way before there are users. It was narrowed to notice periods before it merged.
- On the Raspberry Pi: screenshots in headless Chromium at 390 and 1280 px wide, before and after;
  `tools/pages.py --check`; `node --check` on the scripts and tests; the ledger checker.
- No new words in any language: each lost the two Keep it plain strings, and nothing was added.
- **The deploy** (run [38153347089](https://github.com/honestrobin/qr/actions/runs/38153347089),
  main `5aba909`, with the 1.4.0 record under it): the checks again, 235 passed, 61 skipped, and
  the same iPhone redraw test passed on its second try.
- **Live, after the deploy,** checked by this release's agent and by 1.4.0's: the page loads
  `qr.js?v=ddc175ae0d` and `qr.css?v=0fdfca44a4`, byte for byte the files on main; the robin comes
  after the first lines of text and before the field; the name at the top is in its new form, on
  the missing-page page too; no page has Keep it plain or the scissors; `/`, `/hr/`, `/de/` and
  `/ja/` answer, a missing page gives 404; `/count` refuses "qr kept plain" (400), as it does a
  GET (405), a count not on the list (400) and an oversized one (413).
