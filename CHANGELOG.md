# Changelog

What changed in QR that you'd notice, newest first, before it ships.

## 1.9.1, 15 October 2026

- The answer to "Can I change the link later?" says what's planned: a paid option with codes you
  can change after printing, and scan counts, with a link to the new roadmap. The free tool stays
  as it is. The roadmap (`ROADMAP.md`) lists what's now, next and later, in order, without dates.

## 1.9.0, 15 October 2026

- On a phone, the code sits under the field, with only the line that says what it opens between
  them, so you see it appear while you type. The robin and its line stand under the code, and the
  paragraph under the headline moves below them. Nothing changes on a wide screen.
- The robin's first line says what it does: "I'm the robin. I check every code before you print
  it." In every language.

## 1.8.0, 15 October 2026

- Six questions, with short answers, under the tool in every language: does the code expire, how
  small can I print it, PNG or SVG, can I change the link later, do you see what I type, and how
  is it free. The answer about what you type links to the page's source code.
- A link to QR shared in a chat or on social media shows a picture: the sticker, with a real code
  for that language's page.
- In search results, the page's title and description use the words people search with in each
  language, such as "free QR code generator" or "QR-Code erstellen".

## 1.7.1, 12 October 2026

- Each layout under Print a sheet shows its size under its picture ("12, 5 cm"), so you can pick by
  size at a glance.

## 1.7.0, 12 October 2026

- One size choice, where you print. The size control under the download buttons is gone: the
  PNG and the SVG come out 5 cm wide in a document (bigger when a long text needs it), and you
  resize them wherever you place them. The line under the buttons still says how small a code can
  print.
- Print a sheet still starts with the layouts. Under them, "Choose your own size" opens the − and
  +: print just one code at that size, or fill the page with as many as fit, so 3 cm or 6 cm
  stickers work again.

## 1.6.0, 12 October 2026

- Print a sheet: pick how many codes go on a page (1, 4, 12 or 30) from four small pictures of
  the page, and the size follows. A sheet no longer prints at the size chosen for the files. A
  layout that would print your code too small to scan is greyed out, and a caption gets the room
  it needs, so the sheet fits one page.
- The print size of the PNG and the SVG is one line under the buttons now, with Change to set it,
  instead of a ruler under the code.
- The line under the headline says it first: free as free gets, with no sign-up.

## 1.5.1, 11 October 2026

- Plainer words, in every language: the line under the headline says what the code does and
  that we never see what you type, and the note on images says what happens to yours.
- The robin says hello in a lighter way, and says it checked your code's squares only once the
  check has passed.

## 1.5.0, 11 October 2026

- Easier to read on a phone: the name at the top stands out from the mat, Print a sheet and
  Colours and image are buttons, and the mat's squares are quieter.
- The robin and its line stand just above the field, so the field's legs are clearly the robin's
  kind. The language bubble and the field lose their white shine, and the sticker its dashed cut
  line and scissors.
- Keep it plain is gone, so the robin stays. A choice to keep it plain made before is forgotten.

## 1.4.0, 11 October 2026

- We count what's done on the page, never what's in it: codes made and taken, and how. It helps
  us decide what to build next. The list is in the README; nothing you type is ever part of it.
  Cloudflare, which already serves the page, keeps the counts; no other company sees them.

## 1.3.0, 11 October 2026

- Choose the print size under the code. The PNG and the SVG carry it, so a document or a printer
  places them at that size instead of filling the page. It never goes below the size that scans.
- Print a sheet: as many codes as fit on one page at that size, with cut lines and an optional
  caption under each. Your browser's print window can also save it as a PDF.
- Drop a picture anywhere on the page to put it in the middle of the code.
- "Scans fine" sits with the code now, apart from the robin, and the robin's line comes and goes
  more gently.

## 1.2.0, 11 October 2026

- The robin's balloon is drawn like the rest of the page, and the robin keeps quiet while a code
  doesn't scan.
- Download PNG and Download SVG sit side by side, with Copy (or Save or share) below them, all as
  wide as the sticker's cut line.
- On phones the field asks in fewer words, so it isn't cut off, in every language.
- Japanese lines break between phrases, Korean between words.
- With Keep it plain, no empty space is left where the robin was.
- The page for a missing address has the robin, and a link to the other tools.

## 1.1.1, 11 October 2026

- The robin stays where it stands: tapping it, a new code or the check's answer no longer moves
  it or the buttons. On phones, the code no longer drops when you start typing.

## 1.1.0, 11 October 2026

- The robin says who it is from the start, and counts the squares of each code you make.
- Keep it plain is a quiet link at the bottom of the page, and your browser remembers it until
  you bring the robin back.
- A calmer mat: small squares, like the ones a code is made of, instead of a ruler and grid.
- The footer links to the Robin's Code, which says how QR is made, instead of repeating it.

## 1.0.1, 11 October 2026

- The robin is on the page from the start, and says its line by itself when your first code
  appears. Tap it to hide the line. "Keep it plain" still sends it away.
- The ruler's marks sit exactly on the mat's lines.

## 1.0.0, 11 October 2026: the first version

- Make a QR code for a link or any text, as you type, and see what it opens.
- The page reads its own code back and says whether it scans, and how small you can print it.
- When something looks like a mistake, the page suggests the fix; only your tap makes it.
- Colours, a see-through background, and an image in the middle: the robin or your own.
- Copy, Download PNG and Download SVG; on phones, Save or share.
- In English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Croatian, Turkish,
  Indonesian, Japanese and Korean.
